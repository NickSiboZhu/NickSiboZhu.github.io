About

I am a graduate student at UC San Diego, supervised by Dr. Kun Zhou and Prof. Biwei Huang.

I believe AI’s progress should scale beyond the knowledge and guidance we provide. To achieve this, I study recursively self-improving agents that turn exploration into verified knowledge and use it to guide further discovery.
